HEXAD TELECOM SOLUTIONS

Job Title: Business Development – Sales Position (Telecom or Utility experienced applicants only)
Location: Midwest, South Central U.S., or Texas
Employment Type: Full-Time (Remote)

Base Salary: $120,000 - $160,000, based on experience, qualifications, and industry relationships.

Position Overview

This is a remote, hunter-focused role responsible for opening new accounts, building a qualified sales pipeline, and converting opportunities into profitable revenue. Reporting directly to the Vice President of Sales, the Director will lead the full sales process, from prospecting and discovery through proposal, negotiation, and contract award.

New customer acquisition is the primary focus. Following an initial award, the Director will remain strategically engaged to strengthen relationships, cross-sell complementary services, and establish Hexad Telecom Solutions as a trusted, long-term telecommunications partner.

The successful candidate will balance aggressive new-business development with the ability to expand customer relationships and position Hexad Telecom Solutions as a broader, long-term partner. In addition to the ability to travel of approximately 40% to 60% is expected

Key Responsibilities

● Leverage your existing relationships within major telecommunication companies (carriers, service providers, equipment vendors) to identify and close new business opportunities for Hexad Telecom Solutions.

● Identify, pursue, and close new business with Tier 1-3 telecom companies (ILECs, CLECs, & ISPs), municipal utilities, broadband companies, and telecommunications providers.

● Identify, pursue, and close new business with strategic hyperscaler accounts. This would include selling tailored solutions in fiber infrastructure, fiber design, testing and splicing.

● Build a consistent pipeline through outbound prospecting, referrals, industry relationships, conferences, associations, and targeted account outreach.

● Develop relationships with decision-makers across executive leadership, engineering, operations, joint use, broadband, GIS, asset management, finance, and procurement.

● Create and execute territory and account plans aligned with Hexad’s growth objectives.

● Lead discovery meetings to understand customer challenges, priorities, budgets, schedules, and procurement requirements.

● Coordinate with Hexad’s operational, technical, GIS, engineering, and executive teams to develop practical and competitive solutions.

● Prepare and deliver presentations, proposals, scopes of work, and pricing recommendations.

● Maintain momentum throughout complex sales cycles and establish clear next steps for each opportunity.

● Remain strategically engaged with customers to identify cross-selling opportunities, program expansions, and additional service needs.

● Build relationships across customer departments to grow individual projects into broader, long-term partnerships.

● Represent Hexad Telecom Solutions at broadband, telecommunications, and joint-use industry events.

● Maintain accurate CRM records, sales forecasts, pipeline reporting, and account-development plans.

● Provide market intelligence, customer feedback, and competitive insights to Hexad leadership.

Primary Services

The Director will develop opportunities involving:

● Broadband and fiber program support

● Telecom engineering and pole analysis

● GIS and utility infrastructure data services

● Joint-use program management and consulting

● Joint-use pole audits and asset inventories

Performance Expectations

Success will be measured primarily by:

● New customers acquired

● New contracted revenue and bookings

● Qualified sales pipeline

● Prospect and discovery meetings

● Opportunity and proposal conversion

● Cross-selling and account expansion

● Growth within assigned markets and territories

● CRM accuracy and forecast reliability

Specific annual and quarterly goals will be established based on the assigned territory and Hexad’s growth objectives.

Required Qualifications

● Five or more years of successful business development or outside sales experience in utilities, telecommunications, broadband, infrastructure, engineering, or related professional services.

● Mandatory: Demonstrable, strong, and current relationships within the Telecommunications industry (e.g., at Tier 1-3 Carriers, Mobile Operators, Infrastructure Providers).

● Demonstrated success generating new business through proactive prospecting and relationship development.

● Proven ability to develop a territory with limited existing customer relationships.

● Experience managing complex, full-cycle business-to-business sales.

● Strong communication, presentation, proposal-development, and negotiation skills.

● Ability to understand technical and operational challenges and connect them to practical service solutions.

● Experience working with executives, engineering and operations teams, procurement personnel, and technical stakeholders.

● Ability to work independently and remain accountable for measurable sales results.

● Experience using CRM systems to manage pipeline, activity, and forecasting.

● Willingness to travel approximately 40% to 60%.

● Residence in the Midwest, South Central U.S., or Texas.

Preferred Qualifications

● Established relationships within Tier 1-3 telecom companies (ILECs, CLECs, & ISPs).

● Experience selling telecom engineering, GIS, field services, or infrastructure-management solutions.

● Knowledge of all aspects of the telecom design and permitting lifecycle.

● Familiarity with telecommunications associations, procurement practices, and RFP processes.

● Experience developing new geographic markets or introducing new services.

Compensation

● Base salary: $120,000 - $160,000, based on experience, qualifications, and industry relationships.

● Variable compensation: Performance-based incentive tied to new contracted revenue, gross margin, new customer acquisition, and account expansion.

● Target compensation mix: Approximately 60%–70% base salary and 30%–40% variable compensation.

● Additional incentives may be available for exceeding annual targets, acquiring strategic accounts, or developing priority markets.
