About the role

Grüns is growing fast, and the ops org needs someone who can bring order to that growth. As BizOps Manager, you'll be the analytical and strategic backbone for our ops team, standing up the frameworks, systems, and rigor to make Grüns Operations Best-in-Class. This is a role for someone who's already proven they can own a problem end to end in a startup environment, and who's ready to do it with more scope and less oversight. You'll report directly to our VP of BizOps and work as a peer to our existing BizOps team.

This role is part of our remote HQ! We have a fully remote, high-trust work environment - and also come together on a biannual basis for amazing off-sites where we can connect IRL.

In this role, you will:
Build and refine supply chain models to improve reliability of COGS and capacity forecasts.
Take full ownership of S&OP and variance analyses, ensuring forecasts and planning are optimized.
Lead preparation for monthly business reviews and OKR meetings, aligning stakeholders and leadership on operational strategy and performance.
Identify, prioritize, and execute cross-functional initiatives that improve key Ops metrics (e.g., gross margin, inventory efficiency).
Develop and maintain KPI dashboards that give real-time visibility into daily, weekly, and monthly performance trends.
Provide supply chain assumptions into the financial model, partnering closely with Finance to ensure integrated planning.
Translate complex analysis into concise, compelling presentations for executives and cross-functional partners.
We're looking for someone who has:
BS/MS Degree in a quantitative or computational field
5-7+ years experience in data analytics in corporate, consulting, or investment banking
Exceptional communication skills with technical and non-technical audiences - concise, solutions-oriented, and able to influence partners through clear, data-driven insights and recommendations.
High proficiency with Excel/Google Sheets and PowerPoint/Google Slides
Expertise with SQL, with R and/or Python knowledge preferred
Exceptional thought partnership skills, able to own direct relationships with stakeholders and build credibility through clarity and judgment
Strong statistical skills and intuition for solving problems in both consumer-facing and internal product areas
Experience with Cloud Computing (Google Cloud) platforms preferred
Approach to the role:
You don't wait to be handed a problem — you go find it, frame it, and bring back a recommendation
You move between deep analytical work and senior stakeholder conversations without missing a beat.
You thrive in ambiguity and resource-light environments — "we don't have a process for that yet" sounds like opportunity, not friction
You hold yourself to a high standard of intellectual honesty — you'd rather deliver an uncomfortable truth than a comfortable slide
You move fast without cutting corners on rigor, and you understand that speed and quality aren't opposites
You're energized by working on a product you believe in and a team that holds itself to a high bar
