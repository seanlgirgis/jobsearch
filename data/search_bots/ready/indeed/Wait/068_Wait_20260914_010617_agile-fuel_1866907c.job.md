Full job description

Our client is an AI consulting and implementation company working exclusively within the Direct Selling industry. They help businesses move from AI curiosity to real AI capability by working closely with leadership teams. Their focus is on implementing practical AI solutions while building the tools, governance, and internal confidence clients need to continue progressing independently.

They are looking for an AI Agents & Automations Engineer with strong engineering fundamentals and hands-on experience building and deploying production-ready AI agents.

The ideal candidate is comfortable working across different AI agent and automation frameworks and understands the importance of reliability, integrations, monitoring, error handling, and maintainability. The role also involves working directly with clients to understand business processes, identify valuable automation opportunities, and turn them into reliable technical solutions.

Responsibilities

AI agents & production engineering:

Design, build and deploy AI agents into live client environments with real users and real business data;
Build the infrastructure around agents, including triggers, queues, error handling, retries, fallbacks and monitoring;
Define the access and infrastructure requirements for each solution, including credentials, permissions, data access, rate limits, service accounts and audit trails;
Design appropriate human-in-the-loop processes where full automation is not suitable.

Agentic AI & automation:

Build solutions using different agent and automation frameworks such as n8n, Make, Zapier, OpenAI Agents SDK, LangGraph, Claude Agent SDK & Skills, MCP servers or custom orchestration;
Select the right framework and architecture based on the business problem rather than defaulting to a single technology;
Integrate AI agents with external APIs, business systems and data sources;
Turn successful one-off client implementations into reusable patterns and components.

Client discovery & solution design:

Work directly with Operations, Sales, Support, Finance and other client teams to understand existing workflows and identify automation opportunities;
Determine which processes are worth automating and where AI agents can deliver measurable business value;
Translate non-technical business requirements into clear, buildable technical specifications;
Explain technical decisions clearly to both engineering and non-technical stakeholders.

Documentation & continuous improvement:

Document solutions and conduct handover sessions so client and internal teams can independently maintain what has been built;
Help establish reusable engineering standards and processes for AI agent and automation development;
Automate internal workflows and identify opportunities to improve how the company itself uses AI.
‍

Requirements
Strong software engineering fundamentals with hands-on experience in Python or TypeScript;
Demonstrated experience building and deploying AI agents / agentic workflows in production, not only prototypes or sandbox projects;
Hands-on experience with several agent or automation technologies such as n8n, Make, Zapier, OpenAI Agents SDK, LangGraph, Claude Agent SDK & Skills, MCP servers or custom orchestration;
Confident working with APIs, integrations and webhooks;
Comfortable with production engineering topics including authentication, scopes, secrets management, logging, monitoring, cost control and API limitations;
Enough infrastructure knowledge to independently deploy and maintain the solutions you build;
Able to demonstrate 2–3 AI agents you have built and deployed with measurable business outcomes such as hours saved, reduced error rates, improved cycle times or revenue impact;
Strong client-facing and discovery skills with the ability to communicate effectively with non-technical stakeholders and turn business problems into technical solutions;
Advanced English level;
Pragmatic engineering mindset: able to choose a simple, reliable solution over unnecessary technical complexity.
Bonus points for
Experience with AI evals, observability or agent monitoring;
Experience with RAG and vector databases;
Frontend development experience sufficient to build usable interfaces around AI solutions;
Experience with multi-agent orchestration;
Security, data protection or compliance experience;
Previous experience in an agency or consultancy environment, working across multiple client projects;
Direct Selling industry experience is a plus, but not required.
We offer excellent benefits, including but not limited to
The chance to join a fast-growing AI consultancy at the point it's defining a category, with real client exposure from day one;
A permanent, fully remote role with a basic salary and the potential to participate in the share option scheme;
A seat at the table on how we evolve our methodology, our tooling and the way we work with clients. We want you shaping the playbook, not just following it.
