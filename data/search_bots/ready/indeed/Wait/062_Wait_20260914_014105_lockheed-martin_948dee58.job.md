Standard Job Description

The Lockheed Martin Enterprise Business & Business Transformation team seeks an Engineering Application Infrastructure Analyst for the Engineering Enablement team with focus on the Dassault 3DExperience platform. The analyst will develop, modify, test, deploy, and maintain enterprise‑wide PLM applications for Rotary & Mission Systems (RMS) and key partners. Daily duties include collaborating with team members and internal customers on various application initiatives, projects, and tool suites while ensuring a stable PLM environment, and planning, implementing, and coordinating system upgrades, enhancements, and maintenance.


Responsibilities include, but are not limited to:

Engineering applications project execution and sustainment
Engineering tools operational support
Functional and technical design
Apply technical expertise to design, develop and deploy innovative solutions via automation and Infrastructure-as-code.
Work within an Agile product team, taking objectives from the product owner, breaking them down into detailed increments, and delivering committed capability on-time.
Implement OS Identity and Access Management strategies to ensure least privilege access and enterprise identity integration
Troubleshoot and diagnose technical issues, communicating and documenting procedures, and recommending architectural changes when necessary.
Ensure service compliance as directed by security team.
Work under pressure, during outages, to debug and resolve environment issues.
Develop standard solutions for high-availability and disaster recovery, backup/recovery, file services, and other OS related solutions.
Vulnerability and compliance management
Full time remote position - prefer location to be near a Lockheed Martin Facility


Basic Qualifications
Bachelors degree from an accredited college in a related discipline, or equivalent experience/combined education, with 5 years of professional experience; or 3 years of professional experience with a related Masters degree. Considered career, or journey, level.
1-3 years Unix experience
Scripting in Python
Familiarity with networking and related concepts (Firewalls, isolans, switches and routers)
Familiarity with Cyber Security concepts Experience supporting business critical enterprise applications
Desired Skills
Experience with Dassault 3DX application installations
AWS, AIX, Tomee, Tomcat, Shibboleth, Bash
Tomee multiple instance environment with multiple CATALINA_BASES
Demonstrated success in implementing process improvements
Familiarity with a wide variety engineering tools and processes
Demonstrated ability to work across organization boundaries to leverage best practices and share assets
Linux & AIX including file systems, process management, memory management
Ability to capture key resource utilization metrics such as CPU, memory, disk usage.
Demonstrated willingness to take initiative and develop innovative solutions to complex problems.
Ability to multi-task and work independently, as well as work collaboratively with teams, some of which may be geographically distributed.
Initiative and ability to quickly acquire knowledge.
Ability to manage ambiguous or competing requirements, collaborating with project teams to align on solutions.
Proficiency with Agile and DevSecOps tools and management principles.
Experience operating in US Government regulated environments with strict compliance requirements desired.
