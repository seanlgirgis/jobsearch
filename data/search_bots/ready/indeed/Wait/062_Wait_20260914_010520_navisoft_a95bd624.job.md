Job Overview

We are seeking innovative and highly motivated AI Engineers to join our dynamic team dedicated to advancing artificial intelligence and machine learning solutions. In this role, you will develop, implement, and optimize cutting-edge AI models and algorithms that drive impactful insights and automation across various data-driven applications. Your expertise will help shape the future of intelligent systems, leveraging big data systems, cloud services, and advanced statistical techniques to solve complex problems. This is an exciting opportunity to work at the forefront of AI innovation, contributing to scalable solutions that transform industries and enhance user experiences.

Duties

Design, develop, and deploy AI models using frameworks such as TensorFlow and other machine learning tools to solve real-world challenges.
Implement natural language processing (NLP) techniques for language understanding, chatbots, or text analytics projects.
Conduct statistical modeling and analysis for research purposes, ensuring accuracy and relevance of insights.
Build scalable big data systems utilizing Hadoop, Spark, and ETL processes to manage large datasets efficiently.
Collaborate on database design and SQL database management to support data analytics and model training activities.
Optimize AI models for deployment in cloud environments using machine learning cloud services like AWS or similar platforms.
Perform model evaluation, validation, and refinement to ensure high accuracy, robustness, and scalability of AI solutions.
Engage in data mining, predictive modeling analysis, and generative AI projects to unlock new opportunities for innovation.
Develop automation scripts using Bash (Unix shell), VBA, or Talend for efficient data processing workflows.
Contribute to the integration of AI models into existing systems with a focus on scalability and performance.

Experience

Proven experience with machine learning frameworks such as TensorFlow or PyTorch.
Strong background in statistical analysis tools like R or SAS for research-oriented projects.
Hands-on experience working with SQL databases and big data technologies including Hadoop, Spark, and Talend.
Familiarity with cloud-based AI implementation using AWS or comparable services.
Knowledge of natural language processing (NLP), predictive modeling analysis, and generative AI techniques.
Experience designing scalable data architectures involving database design and data mining processes.
Proficiency in programming languages such as Python, Java, C, or Bash for developing robust AI solutions.
Demonstrated ability to evaluate AI models through statistical analysis tools ensuring optimal performance before deployment.
Understanding of quantum engineering principles is a plus but not mandatory; a strong foundation in analytics is essential.
