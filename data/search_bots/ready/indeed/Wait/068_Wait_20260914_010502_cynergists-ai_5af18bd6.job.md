Remote AI Solutions Consultant

Cynergists AI | Remote | Commission-Based | 25% Monthly Recurring Commission

About Cynergists

Cynergists is building an AI-powered workforce designed to help businesses automate workflows, increase productivity, improve sales, and eliminate operational bottlenecks.

Our platform combines an agentic CRM with specialized AI Agents that help businesses execute work across sales, recruiting, marketing, communications, websites, customer engagement, and other critical business functions.

We don't just sell software. We work with business owners to identify where they are losing time, money, and opportunities, then deploy AI solutions designed to solve those problems.

The Opportunity

We are looking for competitive, coachable, tech-curious professionals who want to build a career selling Artificial Intelligence solutions to businesses.

You do not need years of technology sales experience.

You do not need to be an AI engineer or developer.

You do need to be comfortable talking with business owners, asking intelligent questions, learning quickly, following up consistently, and asking for the sale.

We'll teach you our platform, our AI Agents, business-process automation, consultative selling, demonstrations, objection handling, and how to identify opportunities for AI inside a business.

Your job is simple:

Identify the problem. Understand its impact. Recommend the solution. Demonstrate the value. Close the business.

Compensation: Build Monthly Recurring Income

This is a commission-based sales opportunity with uncapped earning potential.

AI Solutions Consultants earn:

• 25% of monthly base subscription revenue

• 25% of monthly customer usage revenue

• 25% of custom AI build revenue

Base subscription and usage commissions are monthly recurring commissions. As customers you acquire remain active and generate qualifying revenue, those accounts can continue contributing to your monthly commissions, subject to the Cynergists commission agreement.

There is no commission cap.

Build a Book of Business

This isn't traditional sales where you close a deal, collect one commission, and start from zero again next month. Your goal is to build a growing portfolio of Cynergists customers. As you acquire more customers and those businesses expand their use of the Cynergists platform, your recurring commission base has the opportunity to grow. For example, a portfolio producing $20,000 per month in qualifying recurring revenue would generate $5,000 per month at a 25% commission rate, before additional commissions from custom AI builds. The larger and more successful your customer portfolio becomes, the larger your recurring income opportunity becomes.

What You'll Do

As an AI Solutions Consultant, you'll work directly with business owners and decision-makers to:

Prospect and develop new business opportunities
Conduct discovery conversations with business owners
Identify inefficient workflows, operational bottlenecks, missed revenue opportunities, and repetitive work
Determine the financial and operational impact of those problems
Identify opportunities where AI and automation can improve the business
Recommend the appropriate Cynergists AI Agents and platform capabilities
Conduct demonstrations of the Cynergists platform
Explain complex AI capabilities in clear business terms
Develop solutions and proposals based on client needs
Identify opportunities requiring Custom AI Builds
Follow up consistently with prospects
Handle objections and negotiate opportunities
Close new customers
Manage your entire pipeline inside the Cynergists CRM
Maintain accurate notes, activities, follow-ups, and opportunity stages
Build relationships with existing customers to encourage retention and expanded platform usage
Provide feedback about what businesses are requesting so our development team can continue expanding the Cynergists AI Workforce

How We Sell

We don't want you blindly pitching software.

We use a consultative sales process:

Problem → Impact → Current Workflow → AI Opportunity → Cynergists Solution → Demonstration → Proposal → Close

Your first responsibility is understanding the business.

Technology comes second.

A great Cynergists Solutions Consultant should be able to listen to a business owner describe a frustrating, expensive, or repetitive process and immediately begin thinking:

"How can we automate this?"
