What You Will Do

Support multiple Operations pillars, including Data & Digital Solutions, Hiring & onboarding, Process & Metrics, and Learning & Development.

Analyze data and develop dashboards, reports, and insights to support business and workforce decisions.

Support hiring and onboarding activities, including tracking staffing metrics, identifying bottlenecks, and improving the employee experience.

Help develop, document, and improve operational processes, metrics, and standard work.

Support Learning and Development programs, training initiatives, communications, and organizational development activities.

Identify opportunities to streamline work through automation, digital tools, and process improvements.

Collaborate with engineers, business leaders, HR, Talent Acquisition, and other stakeholders to solve problems and deliver results.

Manage multiple priorities and contribute to projects that improve operational effectiveness across the organization.

This position will require up to 10% travel.

What You Will Learn

How Operations enable engineering organizations to achieve business and workforce objectives.

Hands-on experience across data analytics, digital solutions, workforce planning, process improvement, and learning and development.

How to use data to identify trends, measure performance, and drive informed decisions.

How hiring, onboarding, talent development, and workforce processes connect to organizational performance.

How to identify opportunities for process improvement, automation, and operational efficiency.

How to work with leaders and cross-functional teams to translate business needs into actional solutions.

A broad understanding of how a large engineering organization operates – providing a strong foundation for future career opportunities.

Qualifications You Must Have

Typically requires a Bachelor’s in Science, Technology, Engineering, or Mathematics (STEM preferred) and a minimum of 8 years of relevant experience

Experience working with data, identify trends, and presenting findings.

Experience utilizing Microsoft Office, including Excel and PowerPoint.

Qualifications We Prefer

Ability to manage multiple priorities and work effectively in a collaborative environment.

Strong written and verbal communication skills.

Strong analytical, organizational, and problem-solving skills.

Experience developing dashboards, reports, presentations, or automated solutions.

Experience working with large datasets and data visualization.

Experience using Excel (advanced formulas, pivot tables, data analysis).

Exposure to HR, recruiting, workforce planning, learning and development, or organizational operations.

Experience managing projects or working with cross-functional teams.

Ability to translate complex information into clear, actionable recommendations.
