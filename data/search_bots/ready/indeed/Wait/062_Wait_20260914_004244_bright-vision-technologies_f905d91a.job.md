Cloud Solutions Architect – Remote

Bright Vision Technologies is a technology consulting and software development company delivering cloud, AI, data, and enterprise solutions across the United States.
This is a fantastic opportunity to join an established and well-respected organization offering tremendous career growth potential.

Job Title: Cloud Solutions Architect
Location: 100% Remote (U.S.)
Position Type: Full-time, Direct W2
Salary Range: $140,000–$190,000 Annually
Experience Required: 13+ years

Sponsorship: U.S. Citizens, Green Card Holders, EAD Holders, and H-1B transfer candidates are encouraged to apply. We are unable to sponsor new H-1B visa petitions for this position.

Job Summary
We are seeking a Cloud Solutions Architect to design and govern cloud-native solutions across compute, networking, data, identity, and security domains on a major cloud platform. The role partners closely with business stakeholders, security architects, and engineering teams to translate strategic objectives into pragmatic, scalable, and cost-effective cloud architectures aligned with enterprise standards. The ideal candidate brings strong breadth across modern cloud capabilities, depth in at least one major provider, and the architectural judgment to balance time-to-market, cost, security, and long-term maintainability.

Required Qualifications
Bachelor’s or Master’s degree in Computer Science, Information Systems, or a related field.
Eight or more years of experience in software engineering or solution architecture roles.
Deep expertise designing cloud-native solutions on at least one major cloud platform.
Strong understanding of cloud networking, identity, and security primitives.
Experience with cloud migration, modernization, and platform consolidation programs.
Strong understanding of containerization, serverless, and managed data services.
Excellent communication, facilitation, and stakeholder management skills.
Experience producing formal architecture artifacts and presenting them to senior leadership.
Track record of leading architecture reviews and influencing engineering direction.
Cloud architecture certification (AWS, Azure, or GCP).
Preferred Qualifications
Multiple cloud architecture certifications.
Experience with multi-cloud or hybrid cloud architectures.
Familiarity with enterprise architecture frameworks such as TOGAF.
Exposure to regulated industries with strict cloud compliance requirements.
Experience driving FinOps and cost transparency initiatives.
