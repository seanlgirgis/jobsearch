# Job Match - Sr. Data Engineer (Sharkforce Consulting)

## Strong Matches
- Python-first data engineering background.
- Strong SQL for transformation, validation, and analysis.
- AWS experience, including S3-centered data workflows.
- PySpark/Spark-style ETL patterns and large-scale transforms.
- Data quality checks, monitoring, and operational support.
- Experience with telemetry/event-style pipelines and downstream reporting.
- Practical collaboration in Agile environments.

## Partial Matches / Risks
- Databricks depth is moderate (about 1 year practical exposure/study), not deep multi-year platform ownership.
- Entity resolution/probabilistic matching is familiar conceptually and via data-quality practice, but should be framed as pragmatic implementation experience.
- Spark Structured Streaming should be presented as working knowledge unless asked for deep production war stories.
- Public Trust process familiarity should be framed as eligibility/readiness unless already held.

## Safe Positioning Angle
- "I am a strong AWS/Python/PySpark data engineer who can deliver reliable pipelines now, and I have about a year of practical Databricks exposure that builds directly on that foundation."
- "I ramp quickly, document clearly, and focus on data quality and operational reliability."
- "I can contribute immediately on Python/SQL/AWS pipeline work while deepening Databricks-specific patterns in your environment."

## Things Not to Overclaim
- Deep or long-tenure Databricks platform ownership.
- Azure-first architecture ownership.
- Power BI, GCP, Kafka, Kubernetes, or Terraform expertise.
- AI/ML model development ownership (safe to claim pipeline support, feature/data prep, and validation).
