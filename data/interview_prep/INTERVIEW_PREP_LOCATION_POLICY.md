# Interview Prep Location Policy

1. Real company interview prep belongs in `jobsearch\data\interview_prep`.
2. StudyBook is for reusable study/course/lab material.
3. DataCamp folders are learning source material, not the official home for active company interviews.
4. Each active interview gets one folder named: `<Company>_<VendorOrRecruiterIfUseful>_<Role>_<YYYY_MM>`.
5. Each folder must include a README, role brief, spoken answers, technical drills, final review, and sources-used file.
6. Do not duplicate the same prep package across StudyBook and jobsearch. Link/reference reusable material instead.
