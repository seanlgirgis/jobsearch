## What Not To Waste Time On
- Deep Spark cluster admin internals beyond interview scope.
- Claiming Kubernetes, Kafka platform ownership, or Databricks platform
  ownership without direct production ownership.
- Memorizing obscure syntax over practicing clear explanations.

## Positioning Rules
- Strong: PySpark, SQL, Unix/Linux, ETL, production troubleshooting,
  finance-domain experience.
- Awareness/ramp-up: Autosys and Control-M.
- Keep answers clear, honest, and under 60 seconds in first round.
