About Optym

Optym is a leading provider of decision automation and optimization software products for transportation and logistics companies. Optym’s products solve some of the most challenging decision problems faced by world’s leading airlines, trucking and railroads companies using cutting-edge optimization techniques and have created a cumulative business value of over $1 billion for its clients. The Labs Buisness Unit focuses on building next-generation AI-driven products and platforms, working at the intersection of applied research and scalable engineering. We are seeking a Software Engineer – Backend to join our Labs team. This is an individual contributor role for engineers with strong backend foundations in Python.
