Job Description:
Site Reliability Engineer III

Location: Plano, Texas (Hybrid)

Employment Type: Contract

Duration: 12 months

Role Overview

We are seeking a senior Site Reliability Engineer to provide support for enterprise security, workflow orchestration, platform automation, CI/CD pipelines, and cloud platforms. This role involves collaboration with other SRE resources, Product Managers, and Engineering leads to design, build, and support enterprise solutions.

Key Responsibilities
Provide support for enterprise security, orchestration, and workflow automation.
Manage and support CI/CD pipelines and cloud platforms.
Collaborate with product managers and engineering leads to design and build enterprise solutions like Ansible, Terraform, and BMC Truesight Orchestrator.
Perform Linux and Windows administration tasks.
Utilize enterprise logging and monitoring tools such as Splunk and Dynatrace.
Maintain and support systems including BMC Remedy and ServiceNow.
Required Qualifications

Experience: A minimum of 5 years of experience is required in supporting enterprise solutions, including enterprise security, orchestration, workflow automation, CI/CD pipelines, and cloud platforms.

Technical Skills:
Linux and Windows Administration
.NET, Java, Python
Ansible, Ansible Tower, and Terraform
Enterprise Logging & Monitoring tools (e.g., Tivoli ITM, Sitescope, Splunk)
Dynatrace Application Performance Monitoring
BMC Remedy or ServiceNow
Tableau
Work Environment

This position requires a hybrid work schedule with a minimum of three days per week onsite.
