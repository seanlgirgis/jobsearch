About the Role

We are seeking a Data Platform Engineer to join our dynamic team working on innovative solutions in the finance industry. This role will help us build reliable ingestion pipelines, improve data quality and monitoring, and expand the data that powers our product and internal decision-making. This role is hands-on and individual contributor focused. You will have real ownership, work closely with product and engineering, and help shape how our data platform evolves as the company grows.

Key Responsibilities

Build and maintain data ingestion pipelines from APIs, data files, and whatever else our partners expose to us
Improve reliability and observability of existing pipelines
Proactively design and implement data quality checks (freshness, completeness, correctness)
Own the data you build - from schema design to production support
Work closely with product and engineering to understand data needs and tradeoffs
Help evolve our data platform architecture as scale and use cases grow
Qualifications

Must-Have:

Comfort operating in ambiguity and early-stage environments
Curiosity and resourcefulness when working with poorly documented APIs. Able to investigate source data and determine how it maps cleanly into our data model
5–8 years of experience as a software, data, or platform engineer
Strong experience with Python and SQL
A proactive mindset around data quality and reliability
Ability to work cross-functionally and explain tradeoffs clearly
Nice-to-Have:

Hands-on experience building and operating data pipelines in production
Experience with orchestration tools (mage.ai, Airflow, Dagster, etc.)
Experience designing data quality frameworks or checks
Experience scaling early data systems
Why You'll Like Working Here: We’re a small team, which means high autonomy, real ownership, and direct exposure to our leadership team. You won’t be buried in layers or stuck waiting for decisions to get made!

We operate in financial services, an interesting and complex space that brings genuinely hard product questions. If you like solving real problems instead of busywork, you’ll fit right in.
