Hand-on experience with AWS cloud platform (S3, Lamda, Glue, etc.) & Snowflake.
Experience with DevOps, CI/CD.
Experience in Agile methodologies.
Strong problem-solving skills and the ability to take initiative.
Work experience in the financial services industry is a plus.
Excellent communication skills, both written and verbal.
