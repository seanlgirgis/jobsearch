Summary
Infinite Solutions Inc, headquartered at Sacramento, California, focuses on global delivery model to meet the needs of its most valued customers. Infinite Solutions Inc provides services in the areas of IT consulting, development, training and mentoring. Core areas of services include Custom application development; web 2.0 rich internet applications, Mainframe and client server applications, telemedicine, application consulting services.

Infinite Solutions Inc. is looking for a qualified individual to fill the role of a Data Engineer

Mandatory skills

Minimum of five (5) years of verifiable experience designing, implementing, and maintaining enterprise-scale data pipelines and integration solutions supporting production systems.
Minimum of three (3) years of experience performing data operations and maintenance activities in a cloud or hybrid environment (e.g., Google Cloud Platform, AWS, or Azure), including data ingestion, transformation, validation, and lineage tracking.
Demonstrated experience supporting at least two (2) operational data systems, including at least one (1) engagement for a State Department or Agency or another public-sector client.
Proficient in SQL, Python, and data orchestration tools (e.g., Airflow, Dataflow, or equivalent), with knowledge of CI/CD and monitoring practices for data reliability.
Preferred certification(s): Google Professional Data Engineer, AWS Data Analytics Specialty, or equivalent (not mandatory but desirable).
