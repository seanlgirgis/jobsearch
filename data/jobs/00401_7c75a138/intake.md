About the job
Overview

A leading global asset management firm is seeking a Forward Deployed Engineer - Finance & Investments to join a front office technology team supporting investment professionals.

This role offers the opportunity to work directly with business stakeholders from the quantitative investment team to design and deliver innovative systems that power investment workflows, analytics, and decision-making.

Key Responsibilities

Partner directly with front office stakeholders to design and build technology solutions
Develop platforms supporting investment research, analytics, and decision workflows
Build scalable, high-performance software systems across backend and full-stack environments
Leverage modern AI tooling and LLM-based workflows to accelerate development
Translate complex business requirements into clean, maintainable engineering solutions
Contribute across the full SDLC, including design, development, testing, and deployment
Collaborate with global teams to deliver high-impact technology solutions

Required Qualifications

Strong software engineering experience across Python, C#, and/or C++
Experience building production-grade, scalable systems
Ability to work directly with business stakeholders and translate requirements
Strong problem-solving and communication skills
Familiarity with modern software development practices and tools

Preferred Qualifications

Experience in financial services, asset management, or hedge funds
Experience building front office or quantitative systems
Exposure to AI tools, LLMs, or AI-assisted development workflows
Full-stack development experience

Compensation & Benefits

Hourly pay rate: $90-100 per hour
Engagement type: Contract, contract-to-hire
Benefits: LaSalle Network consultants are eligible to enroll in benefits. For details, visit https://www.thelasallenetwork.com/consultants/ 

Work Environment

Hybrid work model (1–2 days onsite) or fully remote

Fast-paced, delivery-focused culture
