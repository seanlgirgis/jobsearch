About the job
CarMax, the way your career should be!



CarMax is seeking a Senior Software Engineer with strong backend development experience to help design, build, and maintain scalable, high‑quality systems supporting critical business capabilities. This role is primarily focused on object‑oriented backend engineering, with opportunities to collaborate with AI/ML and data teams as needed.



This is a hands‑on individual contributor role with expectations around technical ownership, mentoring, and delivering well‑designed backend solutions.



Key Responsibilities

Design, develop, and maintain backend services and APIs using object‑oriented languages such as Java, .NET, Python, or similar
Contribute to system design discussions and help implement scalable, reliable solutions
Own features and services end‑to‑end, from design through deployment and support
Collaborate with product managers, designers, and partner engineering teams to deliver business value
Participate in code reviews and help ensure engineering best practices for quality, performance, and security
Troubleshoot and resolve production issues, with a focus on system reliability and maintainability
Support cloud‑native development practices, CI/CD pipelines, and service‑oriented architectures


Qualifications

Proven experience as a Senior Software Engineer focused on backend development
Strong proficiency in object‑oriented programming and backend system design
Experience building and supporting distributed systems and APIs
Working knowledge of cloud platforms (AWS, Azure, or GCP)
Experience with relational and/or NoSQL databases
Ability to work independently while collaborating effectively within a team


Preferred Skills

Exposure to machine learning or data‑driven systems, including supporting model integrations
Familiarity with AI/ML concepts or workflows in a production environment
Experience working alongside data science or ML engineering teams


Work Location and Arrangement: This role will be based out of the CarMax Midtown office, Richmond VA or CarMax Technology Hub, Plano TX and have a Hybrid work arrangement. 



Associates based in Richmond work onsite 5 days per week. 
Associates based in Plano work onsite 2 days per week. 


Work Authorization: Applicants must be currently authorized to work in the United States on a full-time basis. Sponsorship will not be considered for this specific role.


Featured benefits

Medical insurance, Vision insurance, Dental insurance, 401(k), Paid paternity leave, Paid maternity leave, Tuition assistance, Disability insurance
