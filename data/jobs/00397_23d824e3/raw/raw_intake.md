Position Overview
The Forward Deployed / AI Engineer is the primary hands-on engineer within the Digital IT team. The role takes prioritized business requirements and solution direction from the AI Solutions Architect and converts them into secure, reliable production software. The engineer builds, deploys, integrates, operates, and maintains AI-enabled applications, AI agents, web applications, and supporting services on Azure AI Foundry and the broader Azure platform. This role is delivery-focused and does not own business discovery or enterprise solution architecture.Key Responsibilities
Lead design and implementation of GenAI and LLM-based solutions (agents, RAG, grounding) for internal use cases, from prototype to production.
Develop and maintain backend services, APIs, and web applications using Python, C#, .NET, and TypeScript.
Build and integrate vector search and semantic retrieval pipelines, including prompt engineering and grounding workflows.
Implement and tune LLMs and agent orchestration using Azure OpenAI, Semantic Kernel, LangChain, or equivalent frameworks.
Deploy and operate solutions on Azure using Azure AI Foundry, Container Apps, Azure DevOps pipelines, and containers; ensure CI/CD and automated testing.
Design scalable distributed system architectures, manage state and data flow across services, and optimize for latency, cost, and reliability.
Integrate with SQL and NoSQL data stores, ensure data schema, indexing, and retrieval strategies support vector and grounding needs.
Apply security best practices: identity and access controls, data protection, secrets management, and compliance requirements in deployed solutions.
Collaborate directly with customers and stakeholders onsite as needed, translating business requirements into technical designs and demos.
Monitor, troubleshoot, and iterate on production systems, capturing metrics, logging, and alerts to drive reliability and performance improvements.
Document designs, runbooks, and knowledge transfer materials; mentor and transfer skills to customer teams for sustained operations.
Qualifications
6+ years of software engineering experience building distributed web and API-driven systems; proven experience delivering production solutions.
Hands-on experience with Azure AI Foundry, Azure OpenAI, and Azure DevOps; familiarity with Container Apps or equivalent container hosting services.
Proficient in Python and at least one of C#/.NET or TypeScript for building services and integrations.
Practical experience with LLMs, generative AI agents, retrieval-augmented generation (RAG), and grounding strategies.
Experience with Semantic Kernel, LangChain, or comparable orchestration frameworks and libraries.
Knowledge of vector search technologies, prompt engineering, and integration of vector databases for semantic retrieval.
Strong understanding of security, identity, and access control patterns for cloud-native applications.
Experience integrating and operating SQL and NoSQL data stores in production environments.
Bachelors or Masters degree in Computer Science, Engineering, or related field, or equivalent practical experience.
