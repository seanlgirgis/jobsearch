Summary
The Data Analyst will design and implement robust business intelligence solutions, including ad-hoc reports, dashboards, and queries that drive data-driven decisions. This person will serve as a subject matter expert on data modeling, visualization, and analytics, working with cross-functional teams to identify opportunities to enhance reporting, data quality, and create automation using AI and Machine Learning in strategic opportunities. This position will play a crucial role in adapting new technologies and collaborating in decisions to innovate FedEx Office reporting mechanisms and represent FedEx third party retail needs to enhance reporting that grows and improves network performance.

"This is a hybrid position located in Plano, TX or Collierville TN. Candidates must live within 50 miles of the campus location. Employees will be required to work at the FedEx campus location several times per week."

Lead cross-functional analytics projects from planning to execution
Translate business needs into data requirements with project plans
Lead cross-functional teams of data engineers, analysts, and business units to drive alignment and meet project deadlines
Manage timelines, resources, and budgets for analytics initiatives
Communicate data insights to stakeholders that are actionable to drive business decisions, change management and results
Ensure data governance, quality, and compliance standards are met
Resolve problems and makes recommendations related to complex and interrelated matters and projects
Provide oversight and work with IT partners to optimize functionality, controls, and enhancements for a business analytics platform
All other duties as needed or required

MINIMUM QUALIFICATIONS AND REQUIREMENTS:

Bachelor's degree in Marketing, Finance, Business, Data Science, Statistics, or related business discipline
3-5 years of experience in analytics or data project or program management, or related advanced degree
Knowledge of BI tools (e.g., Tableau, Power BI), and data modeling
Knowledge of Agile or Scrum methodologies a plus
Excellent communication and stakeholder management skills at all levels
Experience leading complex initiatives in a corporate environment.
Knowledge of the broad application of advanced principles, theories, concepts and techniques in marketing disciplines, project management and information analysis. Strong human relations and communication/presentation skills.
Advanced organizational, quantitative and analytical skills with a customer-focused mindset
Experience working with development teams on enhancements, defect prioritization, and roadmap for an IT system
For new hires, must meet all FedEx Office employment qualifications in force at time of hiring
For current FedEx Office team members, must meet hiring criteria for the position and transfer requirements as outlined in the Team Member Handbook
