About the Role
We are looking for an AI Solutions Architect to work closely with clients, partners, and
internal teams to design AI-driven solutions tailored to specific business needs. This is a
customer-facing role with a strong focus on consulting, pre-sales support, and solution
design.
You will be responsible for translating client requirements into clear, scalable AI solution
architectures, supporting proposals, and leading proof-of-concepts (PoCs). The role is
part-time and fully remote, open to candidates based in Europe or the USA.

Key Responsibilities
– Act as a customer-facing AI expert in consulting, pre-sales, and partner
engagements
– Design AI solutions tailored to client business and technical requirements
– Translate use cases into end-to-end AI solution architectures
– Produce architecture diagrams, technical documentation, and solution proposals
– Lead and support proof-of-concepts (PoCs) and early-stage implementations
– Collaborate with sales, engineering, and delivery teams to ensure feasible and
scalable solutions
– Present solutions to technical and non-technical stakeholders
– Support solution estimation, scope definition, and technical decision-making

Required Skills & Experience
– Strong background in solution architecture, consulting, or technical pre-sales
– Experience designing AI or data-driven solutions for enterprise clients
– Ability to translate business requirements into technical architectures
– Experience creating architecture diagrams, proposals, and technical
presentations
– Solid understanding of AI/ML concepts and modern AI capabilities
– Strong communication and stakeholder management skills
– Ability to work independently in a remote, part-time environment

Nice to Have
– Experience with cloud platforms (AWS, Azure, GCP)
– Background in IT consulting, SaaS, or enterprise platforms
– Familiarity with integration patterns, APIs, and system design
– Exposure to platforms such as ServiceNow, ERP, CRM, or other enterprise
systems
– Experience working with international clients and partners

What We Offer
– Part-time, flexible schedule
– Fully remote work (USA time zones)
– Opportunity to work in a customer-facing AI architecture role
– Collaboration with an experienced, international team
– Potential for long-term cooperation and expanded responsibilities
