Description
Data Engineer (AI & Cloud Platforms)

We are seeking a Data Engineer to support the design, development, and optimization of modern data platforms across cloud and AI-driven environments. This role will work across internal and client-facing initiatives, building scalable data pipelines, supporting machine learning workflows, and enabling high-performance analytics solutions. Position is hybrid in DFW area.

Responsibilities

Design, build, and optimize scalable data pipelines, ETL/ELT workflows, and cloud-based data platforms (data lakes, warehouses, lakehouses)
Support AI/ML integration by building feature pipelines, enabling model workflows, and integrating tools such as LLMs or vector databases
Develop and maintain cloud infrastructure (AWS, Azure, or GCP) including services like S3, Glue, Athena, and Redshift
Implement orchestration and automation using tools like Airflow or Prefect, along with infrastructure-as-code (Terraform, dbt)
Collaborate with cross-functional teams and stakeholders to translate business requirements into technical data solutions
Qualifications

5+ years of experience in data engineering or related roles with strong hands-on expertise in Python and SQL
Experience building and managing data pipelines, warehouses, and modern data platforms (Snowflake, Databricks, BigQuery, etc.)
Hands-on experience with AWS and cloud-based data services, along with orchestration and IaC tools
Exposure to machine learning pipelines, MLOps concepts, or AI-driven data workflows preferred
Strong communication skills with the ability to work in client-facing or consulting environments
