Job Description

We are GDIT, one of the largest IT and mission services providers to the government. We offer our customers the power of choice through a vast cloud ecosystem.

GDIT is your place. You make it your own by bringing your passion for accelerating the cloud. By owning your opportunity at GDIT, you are helping to ensure our mission is never interrupted. Our work depends on an Integration Developer joining our team to support the Centers for Medicare and Medicaid Services remotely. Work Visa sponsorship will not be provided.

At GDIT, people are our differentiator. As an Integration Developer supporting CMS, you will be trusted to develop Python scripts on Amazon Web Services (AWS) for a Proof of Concept (POC), configuring and integrating services that will support CMS's goal of replacing "shared systems" with near real-time abilities.

WHAT YOU'LL NEED:
Technical Training, Certification(s) and/or Degree
4+ Years of hands-on advanced experience developing Python code.
Hands-on experince setting up an Integration environment using AWS Glue, S3, and RDB
Hands-on experience with implementing system integrations and interfaces.
Hands-on experince with SQL, data seeding, data mapping and data validation.
Hands-on experince with AWS Lambda development.
Hands-on experince with PySpark.
Experince with AWS tools and applications, including the following: AWS AppSync, AWS MSK (Kafka), and Amazon EventBridge.
Must be able to obtain CMS Public Trust clearance
Must have lived in the United States at least three (3) out of the last five (5) years

PREFERRED QUALIFICATIONS
Experience with Medicare Part A, Medicare Part B, and DME
Experience reading Docker outputs
Experience with Fargate
Understanding queueing mechanisms such as SQS and/or SMS
Experience with COBOL
Ability to manage time effectively, to work under time pressure, to meet schedules and deadlines, to multi-task, to plan, organize and prioritize work assignments and pay close attention to detail
